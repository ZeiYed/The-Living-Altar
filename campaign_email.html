The Retreat

An Immersive Journey Through
Sound, Ceremony & Nature

Living Altar is a five-day immersion that weaves together ancient practices, music, movement, and ceremony to create the conditions for meaningful transformation. Designed to help you slow down, listen deeply, and reconnect with the wisdom of your own heart.

At the heart of the retreat is a dedicated evening of deep inner exploration, gathered around a ceremonial altar: a space for prayer, music, contemplation, and remembrance, followed by a full day of integration, rest, and embodied reflection.

The Facilitators

Guided by Experience
Held with Heart

 Ceremonialist · Voice Artist Zei

A musician, ceremonial space holder and student of indigenous wisdom. Rooted in North African tradition and Sufi practice, Zei has sat with wisdom keepers across the Amazon, South America and the East. With her voice, she carries that blended wisdom into sound and sacred ceremonial space.
Ceremonialist · Musician Nael

A multi-instrumental musician and ceremonial space holder, shaped by deep immersion in indigenous Amazonian ceremony, Eastern music and Sufi tradition. His technical mastery of sound is matched only by the heart behind it — he holds space the way he plays: with precision and warmth.
Sound Healer · Space Host Renato

A healer, sound practitioner and keeper of the land. Renato has walked the path of inner awakening for over 25 years, through meditation, energy healing, yoga and sacred philosophy. He founded Casa Prema and built a sanctuary from the ground up, to offer others what he found for himself.
Musician · Sound Alchemist Art

An artist in the truest sense — always moving, always seeking. He has spent years chasing the world's most rare and exotic instruments, sitting with Eastern masters, and weaving their secrets into sound entirely his own. His practice spans handpan, movement, breathwork and embodiment.
